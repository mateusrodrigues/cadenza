# Chrome Web Store listing (draft)

Text for the store's Store listing tab. Images are in this folder and are final.

## Name

Cadenza

## Summary

Shown under the name; 132 characters at most. Same as the manifest description.

> Writing suggestions in any text field, using the AI provider you choose.

## Category

Productivity. In the store's current list, **Tools** is the closest fit; **Communication** is the
alternative if Tools isn't offered.

## Language

English

## Description

> Cadenza checks your writing as you type, in the text fields you already use: email,
> comments, chat, forms and documents on the web. It underlines what it would change, and
> one click applies the fix.
>
> Suggestions come in four kinds, each with its own underline:
>
> • Correctness: spelling, grammar and punctuation
> • Clarity: shorter, plainer ways to say it
> • Style: weak or repeated words
> • Tone: how a sentence may land
>
> Click an underline to see the change and why it helps, then accept or dismiss it. The badge
> in the corner of the field lists every suggestion at once, with Accept all for corrections.
> Everything works from the keyboard too (Alt+Shift+. by default).
>
> YOUR PROVIDER, YOUR KEY
>
> Cadenza has no server and no account. You choose where your text is checked:
>
> • A hosted AI provider, with your own API key. You pay the provider directly, at its rates.
> • A model running on your own computer. Your text never leaves it.
>
> Cadenza lists the models your provider offers, so you can pick a fast one or a thorough one.
> A connection test shows how quickly it replies before you start.
>
> PRIVATE BY DESIGN
>
> • Text goes only to the provider you chose, and only from fields you are typing in.
> • Password, payment and one-time-code fields are never read. Neither are code editors.
> • Turn Cadenza off on any site in one click. While it is off, it doesn't read the page's text.
> • Your API keys stay in this browser, out of reach of the pages you visit. You can lock them
>   with a passphrase.
> • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
>
> MAKE IT YOURS
>
> • American, British, Canadian or Australian English
> • Choose which kinds of suggestions you see, and the formality and readers you're writing for
> • A personal dictionary for names and terms
> • "Don't suggest this again" for anything you want left alone, with Undo
> • A daily limit on checks, and a record of how many you've made
> • Light and dark themes that follow your system
>
> Cadenza works in plain text boxes and in rich editors. It doesn't yet support Google Docs.
